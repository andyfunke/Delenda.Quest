# DELENDA.QUEST Social Metagame

## Product rule

Friends never increase combat power, production, intelligence, order capacity, success probability, or campaign survivability. Social play creates witnesses, comparisons, identity, authored variety, and collective history. A player who ignores the social layer receives the complete simulation and suffers no strategic disadvantage.

## Two-score model

### Campaign Balance

The in-campaign record remains literal and legible: Forces, controlled kilometers, days elapsed, losses, state condition, and the deterministic decision ledger. These values are not collapsed into a single number while the campaign is active.

### Command Standing

Command Standing is the permanent account-wide score. It is awarded only when a campaign closes and never buys power. The award is calculated from disclosed campaign conclusions:

- Victory or defeat
- Forces preserved relative to opening deployable force
- Ground secured relative to the victory boundary
- Civil state preserved
- Operational problems resolved
- Verified wins and doctrine extracted
- Campaign length
- Difficulty normalization for theater, adversary, and opening-state archetype

The player receives a complete scorecard, not an opaque number. Replaying easy campaigns cannot dominate the record because the award is normalized and repeated conditions have declining record value, while novel theaters, adversaries, and doctrines earn breadth recognition.

Command Standing advances permanent service ranks. Ranks unlock expression and authored variety, never stronger arithmetic:

- Additional campaign situations and theaters
- Quote Canon entries
- Ava acknowledgements and report voices
- Service-record layouts, seals, standards, and dossier stationery
- Shareable victory certificates
- Friend and regiment profile features
- Historical comparison and archive tools

## V1 social loop

1. **Service Record**: a permanent profile containing Command Standing, service rank, campaigns completed, victories, theaters, signature doctrines, rare outcomes, and the player's best-preserved state.
2. **Victory Certificate**: a compact, spoiler-light result card inspired by Wordle. It shows outcome, theater, days, force-preservation band, ground secured, decisive maneuver, and one campaign quote. It can be posted to LinkedIn or any social channel without exposing the decision solution.
3. **Witnessed Campaigns**: friends can acknowledge a completed campaign with one bounded commendation: `PRESERVATION`, `AUDACITY`, `DISCIPLINE`, `ADAPTATION`, or `COSTLY NECESSITY`. A commendation has no score or progression value, so it remains expressive rather than farmable.
4. **Sealed Challenge**: a player can challenge friends to command an equivalent authored campaign. The game keeps the deterministic key hidden. Decisions remain private until both campaigns close, then the game produces a branch comparison showing where the commands diverged.
5. **Friend Activity Ledger**: restrained events only: campaign opened, victory, defeat, promotion, exceptional outcome, doctrine completed, and challenge resolved. No daily-login notifications and no feed of ordinary clicks.
6. **Campaign Reliquary**: every closed campaign produces one nontransferable artifact tied to its actual decision ledger. Rare artifacts arise from rare play outcomes, not randomized purchasing.

## Next social layer

### Regiments

A regiment is a small friend cohort with a shared historical record. Member Command Standing is displayed but not pooled into purchasable power. Regiment milestones unlock collective profile surfaces, a roll of honor, and additional historical briefs. No member is punished for inactivity.

### General Staff exercises

Periodic authored campaigns let a regiment compare approaches under equivalent conditions. Each member commands independently. After closure, the group receives a staff-study visualization of decision branches, casualty tradeoffs, and doctrine. Nobody can donate power or rescue another player's campaign.

### Echoes

After a player commits an irreversible order, the interface may reveal a faint, anonymous historical signal: how many commanders issued another order under comparable conditions. It never recommends the majority choice before commitment. This borrows the feeling of Elden Ring bloodstains and messages without turning the crowd into a strategy guide.

### Global war archive

Aggregate completed campaigns can generate public communiques: most common decisive maneuver, aggregate casualties, rarest operational outcome, or percentage of commanders who preserved civilian legitimacy. The global layer changes the game's canon and editorial presentation, not anyone's campaign arithmetic.

## Crypto-adjacent concepts without crypto

The useful NFT concepts are uniqueness, provenance, collection, display, and portability. Blockchain, wallets, tokens, markets, speculation, artificial scarcity, and transfer are unnecessary.

### Campaign Artifact

Each completed campaign receives:

- A unique serial number
- Game version and content-edition provenance
- A cryptographic hash of the final decision ledger
- A server-signed verification URL
- Deterministically generated heraldry based on theater, doctrine, result, and decisive maneuver
- Exportable image and JSON record

This provides the emotionally useful part of an NFT: *this is the canonical artifact of this particular victory*. It is account-bound and cannot be sold.

### Service medals

Medals are soulbound achievements with disclosed criteria. They commemorate actual events such as winning after an operational collapse, preserving a specified share of opening forces, or winning without a prohibited doctrine. They can be arranged on the Service Record but cannot affect play.

### Quote Canon folios

Unlocked quotations can be curated into a player's public folio and attached to certificates. Quote acquisition is deterministic and permanent. No blind packs, rarity market, or duplicate currency.

### Campaign standards

The game generates a heraldic standard from the player's history. New visual components reflect actual accomplishments. This creates the identity value normally carried by character skins even though DELENDA.QUEST has no player avatar.

## Ethical monetization surfaces

- Paid expansion theaters and authored situation packs
- Historical campaign dossiers and advanced archive tools
- Profile, certificate, standard, seal, and report visual systems
- Alternate Ava presentation packs that do not alter advice or command access
- Physical print-on-demand campaign dossiers, maps, certificates, or medals
- A permanent patron edition with a disclosed fixed bundle

No randomized purchases, paid retries, purchasable score, paid intelligence, multiple obscuring currencies, expiring battle passes, loss aversion timers, activity streak punishment, friend-gated rewards, or monetized rivalry.

## Patterns borrowed and rejected

- Deep Rock Galactic separates class progress from account-wide Player Rank and uses promotion as prestige. DELENDA adopts permanent account standing and prestige unlocks, but not repeated grind for power. [Promotion](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Promotion), [Experience and Player Rank](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Experience)
- Death Stranding makes asynchronous contribution and acknowledgment visible through structures, resources, and Likes. DELENDA adopts witnessing and durable traces, not material aid. [Social Strand gameplay](https://www.playstation.com/en-ca/games/death-stranding/)
- Pokémon GO turns friend exchanges into a Postcard Book. DELENDA adopts the collectible record of another player's place and history, but rejects gifts that create a daily obligation or advantage. [Friends, Gifting & Trading](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/6-pokemon-go/section/60-friends-gifting-trading/)
- Destiny memorializes ranks, Triumph scores, titles, and commendations. DELENDA adopts memorialization and equippable titles. Commendations do not feed progression because tying them to advancement devalues the social signal. [Destiny 2 Update 7.0.0.1](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/update_7_0_0_1), [Update 7.1.0](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/article/season-deep-update-7-1-0)
- Clash of Clans records contribution as Reputation and allows persistent shared progress. DELENDA adopts visible contribution to a collective archive, but rejects resource donations and shared power. [Clan Capital release notes](https://supercell.com/en/games/clashofclans/blog/release-notes/clancapital-releasenotes/)
- Elden Ring broadcasts messages, bloodstains, and player illusions across otherwise solitary worlds. DELENDA adopts bounded historical echoes after commitment. [ELDEN RING Online Game Manual](https://support.bandainamcoent.com/s/article/ELDENRINGOnlineGameManualENG6644aed3d8edb)
- Diablo Immortal layers battle-pass ranks, clans, warbands, currencies, power rewards, and rotating cosmetics. DELENDA can use a legible permanent progression track, but rejects purchasable power, rotating scarcity, loot boxes, and social obligation. [Battle Pass example](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/diablo-immortal/24077219/galvanize-your-resolve-with-iron-gambit), [FTC loot-box workshop](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/events/2019/08/inside-game-unlocking-consumer-issues-surrounding-loot-boxes)

## Recommended build order

1. Command Standing calculation and campaign-end scorecard
2. Service Record and service ranks
3. Victory Certificate with LinkedIn/general share actions
4. Campaign Artifact and Reliquary
5. Friend activity ledger and non-incentivized commendations
6. Sealed Challenges with post-campaign branch comparison
7. Regiments, staff exercises, and global archive
